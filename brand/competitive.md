# Competitive visual review (2026-09-07)

Quality references (not identities to copy): Linear, Vercel, HashiCorp,
Datadog, Grafana, Temporal, StackGen. StackGen is a quality bar for
platform/ops storytelling; OpsDevCode must not become another “agentic OS”
dark-navy product.

## Avoided visual clichés

- Dark navy + neon/violet gradient + giant Inter headline
- Glowing blobs, mesh gradients, circuit boards, generic node graphs
- AI sparkles, orbital rings, play-button “agents”
- Tailwind card grids with 16px radius and soft shadows as the identity
- Logo walls as proof
- Terminal screenshots as the only “technical” texture
- Recoloring one glyph four times
- Literal paving / overpass / toll-booth / truck icons
- Stripe purple (`#635bff`) as “premium”

## OpsDevCode distinctive choices

- **Section-cut geometry** (open joints, planes) instead of orbs or grids
- **Paper/ink** drawing surface instead of navy-neon SaaS
- **Plex Serif + Sans + Mono** as one instrument family
- **Four product hues as plane labels** on one paper, not four unrelated palettes
- **Empty company aperture** so the firm is not a fifth product mascot
- **Diagrams as identity**, not watermarked afterthoughts
- Light-first; restraint over “more AI”
